1.0 Objective

HPLC testing procedure used to quantify the amount of Hydroquinone presence in cosmetic products.

2.0 Scope

The quantification of Hydroquinone applies to cosmetic product that is positively identified to contain hydroquinone.

3.0 Definition
3.1 HPLC : High Pressure Liquid Chromatography
3.2 PTFE : Polytetrafluoroethylene

6.0 Procedure
6.1 Equipment/ Materials

Nylon filter, 47 mm diameter, 0.45 µm pore size or equivalent

Ultrasonic bath

Diluent: 0.05M Phosphate Buffer Solution pH 2.5

Reference material: Hydroquinone Reference Standard

6.2 Chromatographic Parameters

Test Method

Reference Standard Preparation (Reference Standard Stock Solution)

Weigh accurately 20.0 mg of Hydroquinone reference standard in a 20 mL volumetric flask. Add in a few mL of diluent and sonicate to dissolve the material. Make up to volume with the same diluent (1mg/mL of hydroquinone). Calculate the actual weight of hydroquinone by multiplying the purity of standard:

~ 20.0 mg of hydroquinone reference standard x Purity

100

Reference Standard Solution

Pipette separately, 1 mL, 2 mL, 3 mL, 4 mL and 5 mL of reference standard stock solution into a 10 mL of volumetric flask and make up to volume with diluent. Filter each of the solution before injecting into the HPLC column. (0.1 mg/mL, 0.2 mg/mL, 0.3 mg/mL, 0.4 mg/mL and 0.5 mg/mL of Hydroquinone respectively)

Limit of Quantitation (LOQ) Solution

Pipette 100 µL of reference standard stock solution into a 100 mL volumetric flask and make up to volume with diluent (0.001mg/mL of hydroquinone)

Internal Quality Control (IQC Solution)

Pipette 0.02 mL (20 µL) of standard stock solution from part 6.1 and spiked in 0.5 g of blank sample. Dilute both standard and blank sample with 20mL diluent. (0.004% w/w of Hydroquinone).

Sample Preparation

Perform sampling procedure according to PKKK/300/KOS/001 Proses Persampelan Untuk Analisis Sampel Unit Kosmetik

Weigh accurately 0.5 g of sample into a 20 mL volumetric flask and add in a few mL of diluents. Sonicate for 30 minutes until sample is dissolved. Make up to volume with the same diluent. Filter the solution before injecting into the HPLC. Repeat this procedure to prepare duplicate sample solution.

Note: The amount of sample used can be adjustable depends on the availability and condition of sample received.

Procedure

System suitability test

Inject six replicates of reference standard solution with concentration of 0.5 mg/mL. Determine the percentage of relative standard deviation (% RSD) of peak areas. The value of RSD should not be more than 2%.

Sequence of injection

Inject 20 µL of each reference standard solutions from part 6.2, LOQ solution, IQC solution and sample preparation into the HPLC column. The sequence of injections is shown in table below. Construct a calibration curve to determine hydroquinone concentration in the sample. Check the coefficient correlation of the graph. The R2 should be at least >0.9900.

Calculation

Determine the amount of hydroquinone in the portion of sample taken by using the following formula and the amount of hydroquinone presence in the sample will be reported as percentage of weight per sample weight (% w/w):-

Determine the concentration of IQC solution from the calibration curve as constructed in 6.2. Plot the recovery value (%) of hydroquinone obtained on the IQC chart. (Currently using limit Recovery 80-120% for IQC > 10µg/kg, based on Official Journal of European Communities 2002/657/EC)

Results Interpretation

Five different peak areas/heights from chromatogram of reference standard solutions represent five different concentration levels of standard solutions. The peak area of the sample solution that obtained from the standard calibration curve is directly proportional to the concentration of hydroquinone in the sample.

A dilution process shall be carried out if in any case, the peak area/ height of sample solution are more than the area/ height of the highest standard solution concentration (0.5mg/mL), so that the peak area/height falls within the standard calibration range.
