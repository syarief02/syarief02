💡 Petua & Perhatian Penting Makmal (Bench Notes):
  • Tapis semua fasa bergerak (akueus dan organik) menggunakan penuras membran 0.45 µm dan degas dalam kukus ultrasonik selama sekurang-kurangnya 15–20 minit.
  • Lakukan Auto Purge / Manual Purge setiap kali pelarut ditambah atau ditukar jenis bagi menyingkirkan buih udara.
  • Pastikan garisan dasar (baseline) dan tekanan pam stabil (RSD < 2%) sebelum memulakan suntikan sampel kelompok.
  • Bagi sampel matriks kapsul lembut (softgel), gunakan Chloroform kerana gelatin/minyak tidak larut dalam Methanol.

6.0 PROCEDURE

(A) General Operation of <LC Solution>

(For Quantitation Using SPD-M20A PDA Detector and Low Pressure Gradient HPLC Pump)

1. Switch on UPS and Shimadzu SPD-M20A PDA Detector.

Switch on Shimadzu SIL –20A.

Switch on Shimadzu CTO-10ASvp.

Lastly turn on Shimadzu LC-20AT.

Double click <LC Solution>, Click on Instrument icon <Analysis>, Click OK.

Under LC Real Time Analysis window, click on the <Create New Project> icon at <Project in>. Enter project Name.

Go to <File> New Method File

Under Total Pump A Flow, enter the flow rate and % of solvent used.

Tick <Display>, key in the required wavelength, click <OK>.

Right click on the PDA window. Click on the <Display Setting>.

Tick <Display>, key in the required wavelength, click <OK>.

If using oven, click <Column Oven>, tick in the box (Enable oven) and enter the temperature.

▹ a) Turn the drain valve knob counterclockwise by 180° to open the drain valve.
▹ b) Click <Auto purge> tab, enter the value (2ml/min) of each mobile phase and auto-sampler.
▹ c) Click on the <Auto Purge> icon. Click OK. Wait until the purging stop.

Confirm that the <Ready> is displayed and the baseline is checked by clicking <Plot> or <Stop>.

To run a sample, click <Single Start> on the Assistant Bar. Key in the following:

Sample ID: standard or sample.

Method File :

Data File: eg.Abc01

Tick on an Auto Increment:

Vial No :

Injection Volume :

To analyze the data, click <Data Analysis> on the Assistant Bar.

At the Filename window, click the Data icon at the bottom and double click the data required.

20. Adjust the integration parameter in the window by clicking at the column.

Go to File Save Data File.

Repeat the procedure (No. 28) by clicking <LC/PDA chromatogram> and click <LC/PDA peak table>

Print the report by clicking <Print>, OK.

To exit Post Run Analysis, click <X> on the top right.

Shut down the computer

Switch off the LC-20AT followed by CTO-10ASvp, SIL-20A, SPD-M20A and finally UPS.

(B) Precautions

Flow rate of Shimadzu LC –M20AT Liquid Chromatograph Pump should be set at intervals of 0.1 ml/min.

Column back pressure should not exceed 250 kg/cm².

(C) Tips on Using Rheodyne7725i Injectors

Rheodyne 7725i injector can use both the ( partial-filling method) and the (complete-filling method

of loading the loop.

Do not load a sample volume equal to the loop volume.

Load less than 50% of the loop volume (partial – filling method) or 200% of the loop volume (complete – filling method).

Performance Check

Performance Check will be carried out in every analysis to ensure the sensitivity for the instrument is always at its optimum level. It is carried out by performing the System Suitability Test using the Standard solution/ Mix Standard Solution and to ensure that the parameters below are fulfilled:

Tailing factor < 2.0

Theoretical plates > 1500
