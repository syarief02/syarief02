TUJUAN

Untuk memastikan ujian identifikasi proton pump inhibitor (omeprazole dan lansoprazole) menggunakan alat High Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) dilaksanakan dengan cekap dan berkesan.

SKOP

Prosedur ini hendaklah digunakan untuk menjalankan ujian identifikasi proton pump inhibitor (omeprazole dan lansoprazole) dalam produk tradisional menggunakan HPLC.

PROSEDUR

Materials and Equipment

Instrument:

High Performance Liquid Chromatography with photodiode array (PDA) detector

Analytical balance & microbalance.

Centrifuge

Column: Zorbax SB C18, 250 nm x 4.6 nm, 5 µm or equivalent.

Reference standards: Omeprazole and Lansoprazole

Chemicals:

Di-sodium hydrogen phosphate anhydrous

Phosphoric acid

Acetonitrile

Methanol

Deionized water

Glassware and consumables:

Centrifuge tube

Solvent filter system

Nylon solvent filter 0.45 µm

Solvent bottle (1 L)

Volumetric Flasks (Grade A)

Nylon syringe filter 0.45 µm

Disposable syringe without needle (5 ml)

2 ml glass screw cap vial

Preparation of Mobile Phase

Preparation of buffer solution:

Dissolve 3.549 g of di-Sodium hydrogen phosphate anhydrous (Na2HPO4) in 1000 ml of water in solvent bottle to produce 25 mM di-Sodium hydrogen phosphate. Mix well and adjust to pH 7.6 with phosphoric acid.

Measure 1000 ml of acetonitrile and transfer in solvent bottle.

Filter buffer solution and acetonitrile using nylon solvent filter 0.45 µm and solvent filter system

Degassed filtered mobile phase at least for 20 minutes to ensure no bubble present.

Preparation of Reference Standard

Individual standard stock solution

Weigh 10 mg of Omeprazole and Lansoprazole into 20 ml amber volumetric flask separately. Dissolve and dilute to volume with methanol. Final concentration of individual solution is 0.5 mg/ml (500 ppm).

Working standard solution

Pipette 0.5 mL of each individual stock solution (500 ppm) into a 10 ml amber volumetric flask. Then make up to volume with methanol and mix well. Final concentration of Omeprazole and Lansoprazole standard solution is 0.025 mg/ml (25 ppm).

Pipette 1.0 ml of working standard solution into 5 ml amber volumetric flask. Make up to volume with methanol and mix well. Final concentration of Omeprazole and Lansoprazole standard solution is 0.005 mg/ml (5 ppm).

Preparation of sample

Sample handling (sampling)

Sampling process differ between different type of dosage form. Please refer to document PKKK/300/UP/001 (Proses Persampelan).

Sample preparation for analysis

For samples in the form of tablets, finely grind the sample and weigh 1.0 g of sample in a 50 ml centrifuge tube.

For samples in the form of capsule, weigh 1.0 g of sample including the capsule shells in a 50 ml centrifuge tube.

For samples in the form soft capsule, weigh approximately 1 g (± 0.5 g) of sample including the capsule shells in a centrifuge tube.

For sample in the form of liquid, pipette 10 ml of sample in a 50 ml centrifuge tube.

Pipette 20 ml of methanol into the centrifuge tube containing sample in the form of tables, capsules and soft capsule. (*Note: Use chloroform for soft capsule samples because this dosage form usually does not dissolve in methanol.)

Pipette 10 ml of methanol into the centrifuge tube containing sample in the form of liquid.

Sonicate all samples for approximately 30 minutes.

Centrifuge sample solution at 4500 rpm for 15 minutes.

Filter each sample using a 0.45 µm syringe filter before transferring into 2 ml amber HPLC vial.

Injection Sequence

First inject blank (methanol), then inject mix working standard solution six times (SST).

Inject the LOD solution before injecting the sample.

If more than five samples are injected in a single analysis, the LOD solution must be injected once every five samples.

A blank or diluent must be injected before the next sample injection. Refer to Table 3 for an example of the sequence table:

LOD solution must be injected at the end of the analysis.

System Suitability Test (SST) Requirement

System suitability test need to be performed for each batch run to ensure the entire system is working properly. Perform at least 6 replicate injection of working standard solution (25 ppm).

Acceptance criteria for SST is as follows:

%RSD for retention time: Not more than, NMT 2.0%

Tailing factor: Not more than, NMT 2.0.

Theoretical plate (N): Not less than, NLT 2000.

Limit of Detection (LOD) Requirement

LOD solution will be performed in single injection before injecting the sample solution. Moreover, LOD solution will be injected in every 5 sequence of sample to ensure the entire system is working properly.

Acceptance criteria for LOD is as follow:

Signal to noise ratio, S/N is not lower than, NLT 3:1

Internal Quality Control

Inject 15 µl of LOD solution after every 5 samples & at the end of analysis.

Acceptance criteria for Internal Quality Control Check (IQC):

The percentage difference of retention time for LOD solution should not be more than 3 % for every batch of 5 samples.

Acceptance criteria for test sample

Compare the retention time and UV spectrum obtained from the sample chromatogram with that of the working standard chromatogram. The identity of each Omeprazole and Lansoprazole compound is positive if:

Retention time of the compound in the sample chromatogram corresponds to the retention time of the same compound in the standard chromatogram.

The UV spectrum is identical to the spectrum of the standard with similarity of more than 0.999 to the spectrum of standard.

The area of peak detected in sample solution is higher than the peak area detected in LOD standard solution (0.005 mg/ml).

Analysis report

Each sample will have one analysis report. Report must consist of detail as below:

Sample details (Sample name, sample number and batch number)

Analysis date

Equipment parameter including equipment name, mobile phase program and HPLC system.

List and preparation procedures of reference standard use in analysis.

Sample preparation procedure and printed sample weight (for solid sample only)

Interpretation of result (Detected, Not Detected or suspected of specific anti-cholesterol compound)

Evidence of analysis must be attached together in analysis report:

Chromatogram of 1st and 2nd injection of sample (with minimum information of compound name, peak area and retention time).

Chromatogram of LOD solution (with minimum information of compound name, peak area and retention time).

Chromatogram of System Suitability Test Requirement (with minimum information of compound name, peak area and retention time).

If sample is sequence at 6 or more, chromatogram of standard before sample must be presented

Overlay spectrum of sample that have similar retention time with reference standard.
