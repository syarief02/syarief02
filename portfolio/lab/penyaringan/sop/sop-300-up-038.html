PURPOSE

This document is intended to establish a standard operating manual for weighing balance in the laboratory.

SCOPE

This guide shall be used for the operation of MT XP 205 DR Analytical Balance.

DEFINITION

None

FLOWCHART

None

RESPONSIBILITIES

PROCEDURE

Start-up

The main switch of the balance should be ON all the time (Standby Mode)

Briefly touch the <ON/OFF> to switch on the balance. The balance will carry out a short test and is ready for weighing

Move your hand over the relevant sensor to close the glass draft shield doors or by touching one of the two < ⇳ > keys

Set the balance to zero by pressing the < 0 > key

Place the container on the weighing pan of the balance (e.g. weighing boat or volumetric flask)

Press the < T > key to tare the balance. The glass draft shield closes automatically

Place sample into the weighing container and close the draft shield by either touching one of the two < ⇳ > keys or by moving your hands over the sensor

As soon as stability detector icon (the small ring to the left of the weight display) fades, the display is stable. Take the weighing result. Press <🖳> key (Print key). The weight of the sample taken will be printed on the printer

Minimal Weight

For maximum tare weight of 1.0 g, the minimum weight is 0.020 g.

Performance Check

Rujuk PKKK/200/010

Daily Check

Daily Check’ is performed for this instrument internally using the Full Automated Calibration Check (FACT) function available in the instrument settings.

FACT Procedure:

Ensure the weighing pan is free of any object/particle. Clean using the designated brush provided.

Initiate ‘Auto Calibration’ by pressing on the auto calibration button.

Auto Calibration is completed when the instrument displays ‘0.0000’.

Complete the procedure by printing out the result and file into the Log book.

Sensitivity Test

This instruments allows a máximum weight of 220gram, therefore;

First Standard Weigth: 200gram, with tolerance not more than 0.1mg

Second Standard Weight: 100mg, with tolerance not more than 0.05mg

Repeatability Test

Test is performed using a standard weight of 10g that is approximately 5% of the maximum allowable weight.
