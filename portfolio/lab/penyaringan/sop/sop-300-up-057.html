Computer

Instrument operation

Switch ON the battery

Double click <TC Navigator> to enter Total Chrom

At this moment, “Hands-on” button grey-out

Starting the Total Chrom Navigator

Seizing – establishing software & HPLC communication

Seizing – Communicating (L) and Communication established. Hands on available (R)

Preparation

To Start Pump – Click instrument setup

Browse & Choose a sequence to trigger pump

Browse sequence

A sequence displayed Action Setup

Status: Setting up

Status: HPLC Ready / Pump Off Click Hands On

Hands-On Pump (or Purge)

Pump – Adjust the composition 100% of mobile phase.

Key in flow rate 3-5mL/min

Repeat 1 – 6 for other line if necessary

Tighten purge valve and remove

Syringe after completed.

Connect the column to the inlet and outlet tubing (could be not connected)

The flow rate and % composition of mobile phases could be change

Equilibrate the column from by slowly increasing flow rate to 1mL/min or as required by method

Benchmark of column C18 (150mm x 4.6mm x 5µm)

At 1mL of 75% MeOH 25% UPW, pressure redback is 2250psi

Hands On Autosampler Flush Autosampler (perform a few times to get rid of air bubbles in syringe)

Double click Oven.exe

Key-in temperature Enter

End of preparation: Run Clear Setup
