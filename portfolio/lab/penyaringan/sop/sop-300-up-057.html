PURPOSE

This document is intended to establish a standard operating manual for High Performance Liquid Chromatography Flexar Perkin Elmer in the laboratory.

SCOPE

This guide shall be used for the operation of HPLC Flexar.

DEFINITION

HPLC – High Performance Liquid Chromatography

Status: Setting up

Status: HPLC Ready / Pump Off Click Hands On

Hands-On Pump (or Purge)

Remove cover of Flexar Pump. Loosen purge valve (Anti Clockwise)

Pump – Adjust the composition 100% of mobile phase.

Key in flow rate 3-5mL/min

Click Set Value

Click Start Pump

Purge for 15 – 30 mL of mobile phase

Repeat 1 – 6 for other line if necessary

Tighten purge valve and remove

Syringe after completed.

Start the pump for equilibrium

Connect the column to the inlet and outlet tubing (could be not connected)

Set the pump at low flow rate for example 0.5mL/min initially.

The flow rate and % composition of mobile phases could be change

Equilibrate the column from by slowly increasing flow rate to 1mL/min or as required by method

Flush the column for 20 - 50mL of its volume, or follow the regular in-house practice.

Benchmark of column C18 (150mm x 4.6mm x 5µm)

At 1mL of 75% MeOH 25% UPW, pressure redback is 2250psi

Prepare detector

Turn on the lamps to set wavelength

Flush syringe

Hands On Autosampler Flush Autosampler (perform a few times to get rid of air bubbles in syringe)

External Calibration

Click Graphic Edit

Select and open one of your standard file (raw file). Example here is Data001.raw

To optimize the processing method, click <Process><Noise/Area Threshold>

Click & drag the background. Click OK to accept the suggested NT & AT. Manually you can increase these number if you want higher thresholds.

You will notice that most of the noises will not be integrated

Go to <Edit Components…> to name the components.

Move the box to the first peak of your interest and type the name of the compound in <Name> field. Click Next. Continue naming the peaks in similar manner.

The method file is opened. Now you can see your component name and retention time appeared in the component list.

Click Calibration Page

Select <Ave calibration factor> if you only have one standard with repeatable runs, or select <Use curve> if more than 2 standards are use.

Select 1st order for the curve fit type. Choose force the origin if you want the curve to pass through origin without intercepting. Give the name for level field and amount (concentration) in amt field.

Select second component and do the same setting for all accept the amount

Click OK
