Materials and Equipment’s

Instrument :

High Performance Liquid Chromatography with photodiode array (PDA) detector

Analytical & microbalance

Column : Thermo ODS Hypersil 5 µm, 200 x 4.6 mm or equivalent

Reference standard : Lovastatin USP or equivalent

Chemical :

Monobasic Sodium Phosphate

Phosphoric acid

Glacial Acetic Acid (AR Grade)

Sodium hydroxide

Methanol (HPLC Grade)

Acetonitrile (HPLC Grade)

Deionized water

Glassware’s and consumable :

Beakers

Volumetric Flasks (Grade A)

Measuring cylinder

Nylon syringe filter 0.45 µm

Disposable syringe without needle (5 mL)

2 mL glass screw cap vial

Preparation of Solutions

Preparation of diluent

Preparation of 2M sodium hydroxide: Weigh 8 gm sodium hydroxide and dissolve in 100 mL deionized water.

Measure 900 mL of deionized water in a beaker and add 3 mL glacial acetic acid. Adjust pH of the solution to pH4 using 2M sodium hydroxide. Transfer the solution into 1 L volumetric flask and make up volume to 1L with deionized water. Then, prepare a mixture of acetonitrile and the resultant solution with composition 80:20.

Preparation of buffer solution

Preparation of 1M phosphoric acid: Mix 6.8 mL of 85% (w/w) concentrated phosphoric acid solution to 93.2 mL water to prepare 100 mL of 1M phosphoric acid.

Dissolve 4.41 gm of monobasic sodium phosphate in 900 mL deionized water, adjust with 1M phosphoric acid to pH4 and make up volume to 1 L with deionized water.

Preparation of mobile phase

Prepare a filtered and degassed mixture of acetonitrile and buffer solution (65:35).

Preparation of Reference Standard

Standard stock solution

Weigh 10 mg Lovastatin standard in duplicate (A and B) in 100 mL volumetric flask separately. Add 50 mL diluent and sonicate for 5 minutes. Then make up to volume with diluent. Final concentration of this solution is 100 µg/mL.

Working standard solution (for calibration curve)

Prepare 1 set of working standard solution for calibration curve from the standard stock solution A (Para 6.3.1). Dilute the standard stock solution A to get five concentration levels of 0.7 µg/mL, 2.0 µg/mL, 10.0 µg/mL, 20.0 µg/mL and 38.0 µg/mL. Filter with 0.45 µm nylon syringe filter and put in vial. Below are the procedures to prepare the solutions:

To obtain 0.7 µg/mL, measure 0.175 mL of stock standard solution and make up to 25 ml with diluent.

To obtain 2.0 µg/mL, measure 0.2 mL of stock standard solution and make up to 10 ml with diluent.

To obtain 10.0 µg/mL, measure 1 mL of stock standard solution and make up to 10 ml with diluent.

To obtain 20.0 µg/mL, measure 2 mL of stock standard solution and make up to 10 ml with diluent.

To obtain 38.0 µg/mL, measure 3.8 mL of stock standard solution and make up to 10 ml with diluent.

Working standard solution at LOD level, 0.6 µg/mL (300 mg/kg)

Measure 0.15 mL stock standard solution A and make up to 25 mL with diluent.

Working standard solution 20.0 µg/mL (for calibration check)

Dilute the standard stock solution B to get 20.0 µg/mL. To obtain 20.0 µg/mL, measure 2 mL of stock standard solution and make up to 10 mL with diluent. Filter with 0.45 µm nylon syringe filter and put in vial.

Standard Injection and Calibration curve

Inject 0.7 µg/mL, 2.0 µg/mL, 10.0 µg/mL, 20.0 µg/mL and 38.0 µg/mL working standard solution before and after analysis by using different working standard solution prepared in 6.3.2. Create the calibration curves of concentration versus area and ensure the correlation coefficient (R2) obtained is equal or more than 0.999. If the correlation does not meet the required criteria, re-inject the working standard solution until the desired acceptance criteria is achieved. If necessary, prepare a new working standard solution before proceeding with any sample injections.

Sample Injection

Inject each sample as a single injection. Compare the retention time and UV spectrum of the peak in the chromatogram of sample with the RS. The sample contains Lovastatin if the retention time and UV spectrum of the sample correspond to the RS.

Retention time and UV spectrum observation

To observe and check retention time and UV spectrum of the sample and RS, refer to document PKKK/300/UAT/004 (RRLC 1200 Series), PKKK/300/UAT/012 (HPLC Shimadzu) and PKKK/300/UAT/017 (HPLC Perkin Elmer).
