TUJUAN

Untuk memastikan ujian identifikasi anti-kolesterol menggunakan alat High Performance Liquid Chromatography (HPLC) dilaksanakan dengan cekap dan berkesan.

SKOP

Prosedur ini hendaklah digunakan untuk menjalankan ujian identifikasi anti-kolesterol dalam produk tradisional menggunakan HPLC.

Materials and Equipment

Instrument: High Performance Liquid Chromatography with photodiode array (PDA) detector.

Analytical balance & microbalance.

Column: Zorbax Eclipse XDB-C18 5µ, 150 x 4.6 mm or equivalent.

Reference standards: Primary or secondary RS or equivalent.

Preparation of Mobile Phase

Dissolve 1.0 ml of phosphoric acid into 1000 ml of deionized water in the solvent bottle. Measure 1 L of acetonitrile and transfer in solvent bottle. Filter buffer solution and acetonitrile using nylon solvent filter 0.45 µm and solvent filter system. Degassed filtered mobile phase at least for 20 minutes to ensure no bubble present.

Preparation of Reference Standard

Individual standard stock solution

Weigh 20 mg Pravastatin, Rosuvastatin, Fluvastatin, Gemfibrozil, Lovastatin and Simvastatin standard in 100 ml volumetric flask separately. Make up to volume with methanol. Final concentration of individual solution is 0.2 mg/ml or 200 ppm.

Mix working standard solution A1

Prepare a mixed working standard solution of 0.05 mg/ml (50 ppm) of Gemfibrozil and 0.02 mg/ml (20 ppm) of other reference standards.

Combine standard stock solution into one vial as follows: Add 0.25 ml (Gemfibrozil), 0.1 mL (each of the other reference standard) and 0.25 ml of methanol into an amber vial and mix well.

Prepare LOD solution of 0.005 mg/ml (5 ppm) of Gemfibrozil and 0.002 mg/ml (2 ppm) of other standard. Add 0.1 ml working standard solution A1 and 0.9 ml of methanol into an amber vial and mix well.

Preparation of sample

Sample handling (sampling)

Sampling processes differ between different types of dosage form. Please refer to the document PKKK/300/UP/001 (Proses Persampelan).

Sample preparation for analysis

Weigh 1 gram (solid form) or measure 10 ml of sample (liquid form) and transfer to a centrifuge tube. Add 20 ml and 10 ml of methanol for solid and liquid form of sample respectively. Sonicate the solution for 15 minutes at 37°C. Centrifuge at 4000 rpm for 5 minutes. Filter the supernatant layer into 2 mL amber vial using 0.45 µm nylon syringe filter.

Injection Sequence

First, inject a blank or diluent, then inject mix working standard solution A1 six times (SST).

Inject the LOD solution before injecting the sample.

If more than five samples are injected in a single analysis, the LOD solution must be injected once every five samples.

A blank or diluent must be injected before the next sample injection. Refer to Table 3 for an example of the sequence table:

Table 3: Sequence injection for Identification of Anti-Cholesterol.

LOD solution must be injected at the end of the analysis.

System Suitability Test (SST) Requirement

System suitability test needs to be performed for each batch run to ensure the entire system is working properly. Perform at least 6 replicate injection of mix working standard solution A1 (0.05 mg/ml (50 ppm) of Gemfibrozil and 0.02 mg/ml (20 ppm) of other reference standard)

Acceptance criteria:

%RSD for retention time: Not more than, NMT 2.0%

Tailing factor: NMT 2.0

Theoretical plate (N): NLT 2000

Internal Quality Control (IQC)

The percentage difference of retention time for LOD solution should not be more than 3 % for every batch of 5 samples.

Acceptance criteria for test sample

Compare the retention time and UV spectrum obtained from the sample chromatogram with that of the working standard chromatogram. The identity of each anti-cholesterol compound is positive if:

▹ a) Retention time of the compound in the sample chromatogram corresponds to the retention time of the same compound in the standard chromatogram.
▹ b) The UV spectrum is identical to the spectrum of the standard with similarity of more than 0.999 to the spectrum of standard.
▹ c) Peak area of the compound is above than peak area of the LOD concentration (0.01 mg/mL).

If the sample was found to be positive with one of the compounds, new sample preparation should be conducted by second analyst on different date from first analyst.

Analysis report

Each sample will have one analysis report. Report must consist of detail as below:

Sample details (Sample name, sample number and batch number).

Analysis date

Equipment parameters including equipment name, mobile phase program and HPLC system.

List and preparation procedures of reference standard use in analysis.

Sample preparation procedure and printed sample weight (for solid sample only)

Interpretation of result (Detected, Not detected or suspected of specific anti-cholesterol compound).

Evidence of analysis must be attached together in analysis report:

Chromatogram of 1st and 2nd injection of sample (with minimum information of compound name, peak area and retention time).

Chromatogram of System Suitability Test Requirement (with minimum information of compound name, peak area and retention time).

If the sample is sequenced at 6 or more, chromatogram of LOD solution before sample must be presented.

Overlay spectrum of samples that have similar retention time with reference standard.
