0.0 SEJARAH PINDAAN & SEMAKAN

PURPOSE

This procedure provides guidance in carrying out control charting and trend analysis as an essential tool for quality control.

📐

2.0 SCOPE

This document shall be used by Screening Unit, Product and Cosmetic Testing Section, Centre for Compliance and Quality Control, National Pharmaceutical Regulatory Agency to plot and analyze accuracy and precision control charts for Internal Quality Control (IQC) Samples.

📖

3.0 DEFINITIONS

Internal Quality Control (IQC) Sample - a pre-selected routine sample with no or low intrinsic analyte concentration that is spiked at level of interest. It is used to evaluate bias (and precision, if prepared in duplicate) of the method as influenced by a specific sample matrix

6.0 PROCEDURE

Control Charting

Accuracy control chart for IQC Samples

Calculate the mean, m and standard deviation, sd of the accuracy data

For IQC Sample that is prepared in duplicate, choose only one sample randomly, e.g. by taking only the lower or the higher reading always, or only IQC Sample A or B always etc.

Precision control chart for duplicate samples

Repeatability limit = 2 x √2 x sd

sd = Repeatability limit / 2.828

Calculate the central line and upper warning limit from the estimated sd using equations below.

Central line = 1.128 x sd

Upper warning limit = 2.833 x sd
