1.0 Objective

To identify and quantitate the presence of menthol, methyl salicylate, camphor & thymol using GC-MS.

2.0 Scope

This test method specifies a procedure for identification and quantitation of menthol, methyl salicylate, camphor & thymol in cosmetic products.

3.0 Definition
3.1 GCMS : Gas Chromatography Mass Spectrometry
3.2 EI : Electron Ionisation
3.3 Analyst : Pharmacist/Pharmacy assistant/Science Officer

Procedure

6.1 Equipment/ Materials

GCMS

Ultrasonic bath

Glass conical flasks with screw cap

Amber volumetric flask (25 mL, 50 mL)

Nylon filter, 47 mm diameter, 0.45 µm pore size or equivalent

GC autosampler vial

Methanol

Menthol, Methyl Salicylate, Camphor, Thymol and Eugenol Reference Standard

Chromatographic Parameters

◼ Column: DB/BP-624, 6% Cyanopropylphenyl 94% Dimethylpolysiloxane, 30 m x 0.25 mm, 1.4 µm or equivalent

◼ Oven temperature:

Mode : EI

Injection volume : 0.5 ⎧L

Split ratio : 30:1

Column flow : 1.7 mL/min

Inlet temperature : 230°C

Interface temperature : 230°C

MS source temperature : 200°C

Full Scan Range : m/z 50 to m/z 200

Gas : Helium

SIM Mode settings : As below

Standard preparation:

Internal Standard (IS) Stock Preparation

Weigh 20 mg eugenol reference standard into 50 mL amber volumetric flask. Dilute to volume with methanol and mix well (Concentration of IS Stock Solution: 0.4 mg/mL (400 ppm)

Reference Standard Stock Preparation

Weigh 20 mg of each reference standard of interest (Camphor, Menthol, Methyl Salicylate, Thymol) into 50 mL amber volumetric flask. Dilute to volume with methanol. (Mixed standard stock - Concentration of each compound: 0.4 mg/mL (400 ppm)

Note: The weight of reference standard can be modified to minimize the usage of the reference standard.

Limit of Detection (LOD) Solution Preparation

Pipette 2.5 µL of Mixed Standard Solution and 200 µL of internal standard stock solution to 2 mL amber vial. Add 797.5 uL methanol. (LOD solution - Concentration of each compound 0.001mg/mL (1 ppm)

Test Method:

Standard Calibration Curve Preparation

Prepare Standard Calibration Curve by performing dilutions according to the following table for the preparation of level 1 (LOQ), 2, 3 (SST), 4 and 5. The reference standard solution of each compound would have the concentration of 3, 10, 20, 40, 80 ppm respectively

Note: Alternative suitable dilutions to achieve desired concentrations can be used.

Sample Preparation (Liniment/Ointment/Cream/Solution)

Weigh 100 mg of sample into a 25 mL amber volumetric flask. Add about 15 mL of methanol, vortex for 1 minute and sonicate with heat on a sonicator bath at 55 °C for 15 minutes. Cool the solution at room temperature and add 5 mL of IS Solution. Dilute to volume with methanol and mix well. Filter the sample solution using 0.45 µm Nylon filter into a 2 mL vial and cap. Prepare duplicates.

Note: Appropriate dilution of sample should be made if the concentration of sample falls above the linear range of the calibration curve.

6.4.3 Sample Preparation (Patch/Medicated Plaster)

Cut sample into small pieces. Weigh 100 mg sample and add 15 mL methanol into a 50 mL conical flask, vortex for about 1 minute and sonicate with heat on a sonicator bath at 55 °C for 15 minutes. Cool the solution at room temperature and add 5 mL with IS Solution and make up to 25 mL with 5 mL methanol. Filter the sample solution using 0.45 µm Nylon filter into a 2 mL vial and cap. Prepare duplicates.

Note: Appropriate dilution of sample should be made if the concentration of sample falls above the linear range of the calibration curve.

Measurement

Inject 0.5 ⎧L of Reference Standard solution, LOD solution, Calibration curve Reference Standard solutions and Sample Solution into the GCMS and check the chromatogram.

6.4.5 Injection sequence for quantitative test:

System Suitability Test (SST) Quantitation Test:

The Tailing Factor of peaks not more than 2.0

The Theoretical Plate not less than 2000

Inject six (6) replicates of Reference Standard Solution – Level 3 and determine its percentage of relative standard deviation (RSD) of peak area ratio. The value of RSD should not be less than 5 % for all individual reference materials

R2, Coefficient of Determination from calibration curve is not less than 0.995.

(1)Retention Time (RT)RT in SIM chromatogram of the sample corresponds to the RT in the SIM chromatogram of reference standard.
(2)Detection of Target and Reference IonsDetection of both Target and *Reference ions following SIM in sample: *Relative Intensity (RI) of Reference ion is within the maximum tolerance as follows: (1) RI > 50%: Tolerance ± 10% (2) RI 20 - 50%: Tolerance ± 15% (3) RI > 10 - 20%: Tolerance ± 20%
(3)Limit of Detection (LOD)Area ratio of peak in chromatogram of sample is GREATER than the area ratio of peak in chromatogram of reference standard at LOD level in sample. (1ppm, 0.025% w/w)
(4)Limit of Quantitation (LOQ)Area ratio of peak in chromatogram of sample is GREATER than the area ratio of peak in chromatogram of reference standard at LOQ level in sample (3ppm, 0.075%w/w)
