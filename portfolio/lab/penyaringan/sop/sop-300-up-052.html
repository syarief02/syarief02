◈ 1.0 Objective

◼ To screen the presence of clindamycin in cosmetic products using HPLC.

◈ 2.0 Scope

◼ This test method is only to be used for screening of clindamycin in cosmetic products which is suspected to contain the substance.

◈ 4.0 Flow Chart
▹ - None -

Procedure

High Pressure Liquid Chromatograph with a variable visible wavelength detector

Whatman PTFE disposable syringe filter, 13 mm diameter, 0.45 µm pore size or equivalent

Nylon filter, 47 mm diameter, 0.45 µm pore size or equivalent

Ultrasonic bath

Water bath

10ml volumetric flask

20ml volumetric flask

Filter paper

Methanol (HPLC grade)

Sodium Dihydrogen Phosphate (NaH2PO4)

Water used must be Ultrapure water, 18 mega ohm

Orthophosphoric acid 85% (H3PO4)

Solvent: Mobile phase

Reference material: Clindamycin phosphate standard

Chromatographic Parameters

◼ Column : uBondapak C18 125A, 300 mm x 3.9 mm i.d., 10 µm

◼ System : Isocratic

◼ Mobile phase : 0.01M Phosphate buffer solution pH 2.5/methanol with 50:50 (v/v)

◼ Temperature : 40oC

◼ Flow rate : 0.8 mL/min

◼ Detection : UV, 205 nm

◼ Injection volume : 20 μl

Preparation of 0.01M Phosphate buffer solution ph 2.5

Weigh ~10.54g NaH2PO4 and dilute with 500mL of water. Adjust pH to 2.5 with orthophosphoric acid

Procedure

Reference Standard Preparation

Weigh accurately 5 mg of the Clindamycin Phosphate Reference Standard and make up to volume 20 mL with solvent. Sonicate for 5 minutes. Filter the solution before injecting into the HPLC.

Note: The weight of reference standard can be modified to minimize

the usage of the reference standard.

Sample Preparation

Weigh accurately 0.5 g of the sample into a 20 mL volumetric flask and fill the mark with solvent. Sonicate 30 minutes. Filter the solution before injecting into the HPLC.

Note: The weight of sample can be modified due to the availability and situation of the sample received.

Measurement

Inject 20 μl Reference Standard solutions and sample preparation into the HPLC and check for the chromatogram.

Results

Compare the retention time obtained from the sample chromatogram with that of the Clindamycin Phosphate standards.

The identity test is positive if the retention time of the sample is identical to that obtained from the standard chromatogram.
