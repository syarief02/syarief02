PURPOSE

This document is intended to establish a standard operating manual for weighing balance in the laboratory.

SCOPE

This guide shall be used for the operation of Precisa XB 320M Top Pan Balance.

DEFINITION

None

FLOWCHART

None

RESPONSIBILITIES

PROCEDURE

Please ensure the power supply adaptor is connected to the unit.

Switch on the power supply at the mains.

Press ‘on/off’ button on the keypad and the balance will do a self-test system confirmation.

After the self-test is completed, the balance display will show 0.000 g (ready mode).

Remove the top cover (metal plate) from the windshield.

Place the weighing container onto the balance pan and press “T” to tare the balance.

After tarring, wait for a few seconds for the balance to be in the mode again, “0.000 g”

Put the sample to be weighed inside the weighing container and read the measurement on the display.

Performance Check

Refer to PKKK/200/010.

Sensitivity Test

This instruments allows a máximum weight of 320gram, therefore;

First Standard Weight: 5gram, with tolerance not more than 0.0025mg

Second Standard Weight: 200gram, with tolerance not more than 0.1mg

Repeatability Test

Test is performed using a standard weight of 10gram that is approximately 3% of the maximum allowable weight.
