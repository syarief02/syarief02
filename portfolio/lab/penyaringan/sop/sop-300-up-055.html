1.0 OBJECTIVE

◼ The test is performed to identify and quantitate the presence of Fluoride in toothpaste products by using Ion Chromatography.

📐

2.0 SCOPE

◼ This method specifies a procedure for the identification and quantification of Fluoride in Toothpaste products.

Interpretation

Identification test

Observe the peak from QC Solution A injected throughout the sequence and compare against the Retention Time (RT) of Reference STD3 (6.3.3). Percentage difference should be less than 3%.

6.6.1.2 The presence of Fluoride is confirmed and reported as positive/ ‘dikesan’ only when these two (2) criteria’s are met:

RT from the peak observed from sample solution with that obtained from Ref STD does not differ by ± 0.5min

The peak area or height of the sample solution is more than the peak area/height of the Detection Limit (QC Solution A).

Determination

Observe the peak from QC Solution B injected throughout the sequence and compare against the Average Retention Time (RT) of Reference STD4. Percentage difference should be less than 3%.
