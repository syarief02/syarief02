◈ 1.0 Objective

◼ To identify the presence of Antimicrobials (Metronidazole, Griseofulvin, Chloramphenicol, Trimethoprim, Clindamycin and Sulfamethoxazole) in cosmetic products using GC-MS.

◈ 2.0 Scope

◼ This test method is only to be used for identification of Antimicrobials (Metronidazole, Griseofulvin, Chloramphenicol, Trimethoprim, Clindamycin and Sulfamethoxazole) in cosmetic products which is suspected to contain the substance.

◈ 6.0 Procedure

GCMS – QP 2010

Ultrasonic bath

Glass conical flasks with screw cap

Volumetric flask (20 mL, 10mL, 5 mL)

Nylon filter, 47 mm diameter, 0.45 µm pore size or equivalent

GC autosampler vial

Ethanol

Metronidazole, Sulfamethoxazole, Trimethoprim, Chloramphenicol, Griseofulvin and Clindamycin Reference Standard

Chromatographic Parameters

◼ Column : Rxi – 5Sil MS 30m x 0.25mm x 0.25µm

◼ Oven temperature:

.

Mode : Split

Injection volume : 1 μL

Split ratio : 10:1

Column flow : 1.0mL/min

Inlet temperature : 250°C

Interface temperature : 280°C

MS source temperature : 230°C

Gas : Helium

Test Method

Mix Reference Standard Solution Preparation

Stock Solution 1 (0.2 mg/mL)

Weigh accurately 4 mg each of Metronidazole, Trimethoprim, Griseofulvin and Clindamycin Reference Standard and transfer into a 20 mL volumetric flask. Make up to volume with ethanol.

Stock Solution 2 (0.5 mg/mL)

Weigh accurately 5 mg each of Chloramphenicol and Sulfamethoxazole Reference Standard and transfer into a 10 mL volumetric flask. Make up to volume with ethanol.

Pipette 2.5 mL of Stock Solution 1 and 6.0 mL of Stock Solution 2. Make up to volume to 10 mL with ethanol to yield 50 ug/mL of Metronidazole, Trimethoprim, Griseofulvin & Clindamycin and 300 ug/mL of Chloramphenicol & Sulfamethoxazole.

Weigh accurately 2 mg each of Metronidazole & Griseofulvin Reference Standard and transfer into a 10 mL volumetric flask, separately. Make up to volume with ethanol. Pipette 0.5 mL of the solution and make up to volume to 10 mL with ethanol to yield 10 mcg/mL Metronidazole & Griseofulvin.

Weigh accurately 5 mg of Chloramphenicol Reference Standard and transfer into a 5 mL volumetric flask. Make up to volume with ethanol. Pipette 1 mL of the solution and make up to volume to 10 mL with ethanol to yield 100 mcg/mL Chloramphenicol.

Weigh accurately 4 mg of Clindamycin Reference Standard and transfer into a 10 mL volumetric flask. Make up to volume with ethanol. Pipette 1 mL of the solution and make up to volume to 10 mL with ethanol to yield 40 mcg/mL Clindamycin.

Weigh accurately 2.5 mg of Trimethoprim Reference Standard and transfer into a 10 mL volumetric flask. Make up to volume with ethanol. Pipette 2 mL of the solution and make up to volume to 10 mL with ethanol to yield 50 mcg/mL Trimethoprim.

Weigh accurately 5 mg of Sulfamethoxazole Reference Standard and transfer into a 10 mL volumetric flask. Make up to volume with ethanol. Pipette 6 mL of the solution and make up to volume to 10 mL with ethanol to yield 300 ug/mL of Sulfamethoxazole.

Note:

Concentration of reference standard solution can be modified as needed, depending on the availability of the standards & response of the instrument

Mix Reference Standard Solution is prepared for reference in GC-MS data library. The individual standard is freshly prepared only when necessary (ie. when sample fails and requires repetition). Otherwise referencing is done from the GC-MS data library. Standard solution must be prepared individually.

Sample Preparation

Weigh accurately 0.5 g of sample into a glass conical flask. Add in 20 mL of ethanol. Sonicate for 30 minutes. Filter the solution before injecting into the GCMS.

Note: Further dilution of sample can be done if the concentration of sample is too high.

Procedure

Note: Only standard solution of the compound of interest is injected into the instrument during repeat test for failed samples.
