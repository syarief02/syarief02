1.0 Objective

The test is performed to identify the presence of p-phenylenediamine and quantify the amount present in hair care products using HPLC.

Scope

This method describes the determination of p-Phenylenediamine in hair care products.

Procedure

6.1 Test Method for Identification
6.1.1 Standard Stock Preparation 0.8 mg/mL (freshly prepared)

Weigh approximately 8 mg of p-Phenylenediamine standard into a 100 mL volumetric flask (amber) Class A. Dissolve the standard in diluent and fill up to mark and mix.

6.1.2 Working standard / LOQ preparation (0.024 mg/mL)

Transfer by pipette 3 mL stock solution in a 10 mL volumetric flask, fill up to the mark with diluent and mix.

Note: The preparation can be modified as long as the final concentration is achieved.

System Suitability

Tailing factor should be NMT 2.0

Theoretical plates should be NLT 1500

Test Method for Quantitation

Standard Stock Preparation 0.8 mg/mL (freshly prepared)

Weigh approximately 80 mg of p-Phenylenediamine standard into a 100 mL volumetric flask (amber) Class A. Dissolve the standard in diluent and fill up to mark and mix. Prepare calibration standard solution as following:

Note: The preparation can be modified as long as the final concentration is achieved.

Sequence of Injection

System Suitability Test (SST)

Relative Standard Deviation (RSD) of retention time NMT 2.0 %

Tailing factor should be NMT 2.0

Theoretical plates should be NLT 1500

Sample Preparation

Weigh about 0.5 g sample into a 25 mL volumetric flask (amber) Class A. For quantitation purpose, perform duplication of sample.

Make up to the mark with diluent and mix well.

Heat the solution in water bath at around 60 ± 1 ºC for about 5 minutes.

Shake the sample gently for 1 minute and sonicate for about 5 minutes and allow the sample to cool to room temperature.

Filter the sample solution/suspension through 0.45 μm PTFE membrane filter into an amber vial.

Perform the HPLC determination within 24 hours after preparation of the samples.

Note: Due to the nature of sample (easily oxidised), sample weight and dilution factor may be adjusted depending on concentration of p-Phenylenediamine in the sample. Concentration of sample should fall within the standard calibration range.

Sample Spike Preparation (IQC)

7.5 mL of p-Phenylenediamine standard stock solution (6.1.1) is pre-spiked into about 0.5 g of sample blank. The sample is extracted in a similar manner as steps stated in section 6.2. Filter the solution before injecting into the HPLC. IQC is to be injected before and after samples throughout the sequence. Recovery of standard should be within 80 % - 120 % in comparison to area from Std B.

Result Interpretation:

Compare the retention time and spectrum obtained from the sample chromatogram with that of p-Phenylenediamine standard solution chromatogram.

The identity of p-Phenylenediamine is positive if the retention time and the UV spectrum of sample is similar to that obtained from the standard solution.

Calculations

Plot a calibration curve and determine the amount of p-Phenylenediamine in %w/w.
