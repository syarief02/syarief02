💡 Petua & Perhatian Penting Makmal (Bench Notes):
  • Tapis semua fasa bergerak (akueus dan organik) menggunakan penuras membran 0.45 µm dan degas dalam kukus ultrasonik selama sekurang-kurangnya 15–20 minit.
  • Lakukan Auto Purge / Manual Purge setiap kali pelarut ditambah atau ditukar jenis bagi menyingkirkan buih udara.
  • Pastikan garisan dasar (baseline) dan tekanan pam stabil (RSD < 2%) sebelum memulakan suntikan sampel kelompok.
  • Bagi sampel matriks kapsul lembut (softgel), gunakan Chloroform kerana gelatin/minyak tidak larut dalam Methanol.

1.0 OBJECTIVE

 The test is performed to identify the presence of Hydroquinone in cosmetic cream

products by using HPLC.

📐

2.0 SCOPE

 The screening method is used for identification of Hydroquinone in cosmetic

cream products received from customers such as Surveillance & Complaint

Section, Pharmacy Enforcement and others.

IQC – Internal Quality Control

LOD – Limit of Detection

OOS – Out of Specification

Blank sample - Sample which has been tested before and does not contain

Hydroquinone

6.0 PROCEDURE

 High Pressure Liquid Chromatograph with photo diode array detector

 Ultrasonic bath

 20 mL Volumetric flasks

 50 mL capped Erlenmeyer flasks

 Measuring cylinder

 Nylon filter, 47 mm diameter, 0.45 µm pore size or equivalent

 Methanol (HPLC grade)

 Potassium Dihydrogen Phosphate (KH2PO4)

 Water (Ultrapure water, 18 mega ohm)

 Orthophosphoric acid 85% (H3PO4)

 Diluent: 0.05 M Phosphate Buffer Solution pH 2.5

 Reference material: Hydroquinone Reference Standard

 Column

: C18 (Cosmosil 5 C18 AR-II), 250 mm x 4.6 mm i.d., 5 µm

 System : Isocratic

 Flow rate

 Detection

: UV, 280 nm
